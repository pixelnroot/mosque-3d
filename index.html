  • Minaret, 4'-0" across, in white and gold, on the roof over the north-west corner column, with four loudspeakers and a gold new moon and star.
  • Roof edge projects 6" beyond every wall so rain does not run onto the windows.

  • Traffic keeps to the left (British rule). People on the walkway wear white thobes.
  • East front wall 10'-0" from the entrance, with a walking path outside it running north to south.
  • East-west road 10'-0" north of the ablution wall, with moving vehicles.

Style from the photo: cream walls, white plinth and cornice, crenellated parapet, arched windows with white frames and bars, dark wooden double door, gold crescent on a tiered minaret. Storey heights are assumed (roof at 12'-0").
